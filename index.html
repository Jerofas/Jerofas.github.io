Questions

Do I need to pay before I see anything?

No. I make a free sample homepage first. You pay the 50% deposit only when you want me to build the full website.

How long does it take?

Usually about 5 days after I receive your deposit and your photos and information.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Every website is built for phones first, because most of your customers search on their phones.

Can customers book or order through WhatsApp?

Yes. The WhatsApp button opens a chat with a message already written, so customers can contact you in one tap.

How do I pay?

By MTN Mobile Money or Airtel Money. I send an invoice and a receipt for every payment.

Can I change things later?

Yes. Changes are included for 14 days after delivery. After that you can use the monthly Care Plan or pay per update.
